CHARACTER STUDY

"I used to think that my life was a tragedy, but now I realize, it’s a comedy."

Arthur Fleck, a man disregarded by society, is a failed comedian who seeks connection as he walks the streets of Gotham City. Over time, Arthur begins a slow descent into madness as he transforms into the criminal mastermind known as the Joker.

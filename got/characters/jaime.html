Jaime Lannister

Character Bio

Ser Jaime Lannister, known as the Kingslayer, begins as a symbol of arrogance and privilege. Yet behind the reputation stands a knight shaped by impossible choices and lasting consequences.

His arc moves between honor and guilt, loyalty and redemption, making him one of the most layered warriors in the Seven Kingdoms.

"The things we do for love."

"By what right does the wolf judge the lion?"

Jaime's identity is tested each season: as a brother, a father, and a knight fighting to reclaim his own name. His strengths in battle are matched by a growing struggle to define what honor truly means.

Few characters in Westeros evolve as dramatically between legend and humanity.

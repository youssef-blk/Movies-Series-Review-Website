Cersei Lannister

Character Bio

Cersei Lannister is one of the most influential and feared figures in the Seven Kingdoms. Born into House Lannister, she was raised in wealth and political ambition, yet constantly measured against power structures ruled by men.

Her worldview is shaped by survival, legacy, and the belief that mercy is weakness. Every alliance and betrayal serves one mission: secure her bloodline and keep control of the Iron Throne.

"I would do things for my family that you can't imagine."

"What good is power if you cannot protect the ones you love?"

Cersei learns to weaponize fear when diplomacy fails. While many see only cruelty, her arc also reveals grief, paranoia, and the burden of ruling in a world where trust rarely survives.

Her rise and fall remain among the most iconic character-driven conflicts in Game of Thrones.

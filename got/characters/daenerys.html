Daenerys Targaryen

Character Bio

Daenerys Stormborn starts as an exiled princess and grows into one of the most formidable leaders in the world. With dragons at her side, she builds a vision of justice and liberation.

Her rise is fueled by courage, destiny, and conviction, but power in Westeros always comes with a price.

"I am the dragon's daughter."

"I will take what is mine, with fire and blood."

Daenerys inspires devotion across continents, yet her journey also exposes the danger of absolute certainty. The same fire that frees can also destroy.

Her character stands at the center of one of the most debated power arcs in modern television.

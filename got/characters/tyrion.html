Tyrion Lannister

Character Bio

Tyrion Lannister is the youngest child of House Lannister and one of the sharpest minds in Westeros. While dismissed by many for his stature, he survives through intelligence, wit, and political instinct.

Across shifting alliances, Tyrion becomes an advisor, strategist, and moral counterweight in a world ruled by fear. His journey is a story of resilience against prejudice and power.

"I drink and I know things."

"Never forget what you are. The rest of the world will not."

Though often underestimated, Tyrion consistently outmaneuvers those who rely only on brute force. His words can be as deadly as any sword, and his loyalty is earned, never bought.

In the game of thrones, he represents reason, adaptability, and the power of a mind that refuses to bow.

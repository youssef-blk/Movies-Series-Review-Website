Peaky Blinders

Language : English | 5 Seasons | 2013~2019

Follows the Shelby family as they rise to power in post-war Birmingham, led by the cold and strategic Tommy Shelby, navigating a brutal world of crime, politics, and betrayal where every move brings both greater control and heavier consequences.

Stars: Cillian Murphy, Paul Anderson, Helen McCrory, Sophie Rundle, Finn Cole, Tom Hardy
Creator: Steven Knight

By order of the Peaky Blinders.

Set in Birmingham after World War I, Peaky Blinders follows Tommy Shelby and his family as they build power through crime, strategy, and political influence.

Created by Steven Knight, the series blends historical tension, gang rivalry, and family loyalty across six seasons, making it one of the most iconic British crime dramas.
